{{<Intro>}}
CS Grad Seminar is a biweekly held event organized by Graduate Computer Science Society. It is meant to provide a safe and low-stakes environment for graduate students to practice their presentation skills.<br><br>
Each talk will take 30 minutes followed by 10 minutes for questions from the audience. At the end, there will be a 20 minute window for socializing and (hopefully) refreshments.<br><br>
Seminars are designed around a general CS audience, so that it is understandable by all CS graduate students. They also provide everyone an opportunity to see ongoing research in the CS department outside of one's research area.<br><br>
{{</Intro>}}

## When will it start?

Hopefully, this semester! We are still trying to find a time slot that will work for the most of us.

## Why would I apply to be a speaker?

You get to present a project you were a part of to other fellow graduate students and get feedback from them. Being able to explain your research problem without too many field specific jargon is a skill on its own. You may use these seminars as preperation before travelling to some big name conference somewhere else.

You can totally include these seminars in your CV as an **_invited speaker_** with a link to your talk in this website.

## Why should I attend these seminars?

There are not a lot events in the CS department where students from all labs come together. We can't keep holding these seminars if graduate students don't attend them! We need you :)

Social aspects aside, being exposed to research outside of your own field is an important part of academia. There is a nonzero chance that you will find your next research colloborator in these seminars.
