## About the competition
The Graduate Computer Science Society (GCSS) invites CS graduate students to design the society's official logo. The winning design will become GCSS's visual identity.
The logo will be used on the GCSS website, social media, event posters, presentation slides, and merchandise such as stickers, t-shirts and hoodies.

## Eligibility
The competition is open only to University of Manitoba Computer Science graduate students.
- You must be registered in a graduate program (MSc or PhD) in the Department of Computer Science at the time of submission.
- Current GCSS executive members are not eligible to participate in the competition.
- You may participate individually or in a team of up to two eligible students, and each individual or team may submit up to 2 designs. A winning team will share the prize.

## Design requirements
Your logo must represent GCSS, work at every size, and be your own original design.
- Concept: The logo should represent GCSS and reflect computer science and the graduate student community.
- Symbols and imagery: You may use any symbol, animal or image, but do not copy or closely imitate existing logos of the University of Manitoba (including its athletics logos) or of other UofM societies and student groups.
- Scalable: The logo must read clearly at 32 × 32 px (favicon, Discord or GitHub avatar) and at large sizes (banners, hoodies).
- Colour: Use colours from the Gruvbox palette (gruvbox.org). The logo should also work in a single colour and on both light and dark backgrounds.
- No third-party content: Do not include other organizations' logos or trademarks (for example, programming-language, company or platform logos).
- Appropriate: No offensive, discriminatory or political content.
- Original: The design must not copy or closely resemble an existing logo. Entries may be checked with reverse image search.

## Use of AI tools (fine if disclosed)
AI tools (for example, ChatGPT, Midjourney or Adobe Firefly) are allowed at any stage, including generating the design. Using AI does not affect your score. The following conditions apply:
- Disclose it: If you used AI tools, list them on the submission form and briefly explain how you used them.
- Check the terms: Make sure the tool’s terms of use allow you to use the output commercially and transfer it to GCSS.
- Submit an editable file: AI-generated images must be converted into an SVG file before submission. An AI image on its own will not be accepted.
- No copying: AI output must not reproduce existing logos, trademarks or copyrighted artwork.
- We encourage you to refine AI output by hand. Purely AI-generated images may not be protected by copyright, which weakens GCSS's ownership of the logo.

## Submission requirements
Submit each design through [GCSS Logo Competition Form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/xwfNZ2zxxn) by the deadline in the Timeline. Each submission must include:
- The logo as an SVG file.
- The logo as a PNG with a transparent background, at least 2000 px wide.
A design rationale of up to 200 words explaining the idea behind the logo.
- The completed submission form: name, student number, UofM email, program (MSc or PhD), AI disclosure and the participant declaration.
- Do not put your name, signature or watermark on the design. Judging is anonymous.
- Name your files GCSS_Logo_LastName_1 (use \_2 for a second design).

## Judging
The winner is chosen in two stages.
- Screening: The GCSS executive checks each submission for eligibility and rule compliance, then removes identifying details.
- Student scoring: All eligible designs, along with their design rationales, are shared with University of Manitoba CS graduate students for scoring. Each student rates every design from 1 to 5 on each criterion below, using a form that requires a UofM login. The design with the highest weighted average score wins.

{{< responsive_table >}}
| Criterion | What the panel looks for | Weight |
| ------ | ------ | ----------- |
| Relevance | Represents GCSS, computer science and the graduate community | 30% |
| Originality | A distinctive idea, not a cliché or a look-alike | 25% |
| Versatility | Works at small and large sizes, and on light and dark backgrounds | 15% |
| Visual appeal | Balanced, clean and professional | 15% |
| Rationale | A clear explanation of the concept | 15% |

## Ownership and intellectual property
GCSS will own the winning design. The rules below apply to the winning design and all other submissions.
- Your work: By submitting, you confirm that the design is your own work, or that you hold the rights to every element in it, and that it does not infringe anyone else’s rights.
- Transfer to GCSS: The winner (or both members of a winning team) assigns all copyright and other intellectual property rights in the winning design to GCSS and signs a short assignment form.
- Changes to the design: The winner agrees that GCSS may modify, recolour or adapt the logo, ask for revisions, and use it without crediting the designer each time. (This acts as a waiver of the designer’s moral rights under Canadian copyright law.)
- Credit and portfolio: GCSS will credit the designer on its website. The winner may show the design in a personal portfolio.
- Source files: The winner must provide the original, editable source files.
- Other submissions: Participants keep ownership of designs that do not win. GCSS may display them anonymously for voting. After the competition, GCSS may share them with the designer’s name to promote the competition. Participants who prefer not to have their design shared can let us know on the submission form.

## General terms

- GCSS may disqualify any submission that breaks these rules.
- All decisions made by GCSS regarding the competition are final.
- If the winner does not respond within 14 days of being notified, GCSS may select another winner.
- GCSS may change the timeline or these rules and will announce any changes to all participants.
- Personal information collected will be used only to run this competition.
