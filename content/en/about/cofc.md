**TLDR: Be a decent human being and don’t be mean.**

The University of Manitoba Graduate Computer Science Society (GCSS) is committed to creating an equitable, diverse and inclusive environment for all GCSS members. We expect all members and outside participants to conduct themselves in a responsible manner and abide by the GCSS Code of Conduct at any GCSS event or online spaces. We expect everyone to treat other members with respect and dignity and to not participate in the discrimination, harassment, or prejudice of another person or group of persons.

## Where, when and to whom does the code apply?

- The Code of Conduct applies to all members of the GCSS and all non-member participants of GCSS events and spaces.
- The Code of Conduct applies to GCSS events and meetings taking place both in-person and online.
- The Code of Conduct applies to GCSS group chats, social media, and any other online space associated with GCSS.

GCSS does not tolerate behaviour that discriminates against individual characteristics such as race, age, religion, sex, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, disability, nationality, educational background, or any characteristic protected by law not listed. It is almost impossible to list every single thing one should not be doing. Use your common sense to avoid conflicts with other GCSS members. Some conflicts are bound to happen. Handle such cases in a civilised manner or simply agree to disagree. If you believe the situation is getting out of control, reach out to the GCSS executive team or other GCSS members that you believe could help. It is preferable to handle such conflicts inside GCSS and bring them to the department level only if necessary.

All GCSS members should make a reasonable effort to attend academic GCSS events, even those outside of one’s main research area. GCSS can only continue its operations with the participation and involvement of CS graduate students. GCSS members need not serve in the executive committee to help/organise GCSS events. Either big or small, all contributions count!

## How can I report non-compliance?

Contact a member of the GCSS executive team if you believe someone has violated the code of conduct. Provide details such as when and where it happened and who was involved.

In the case of severe violations, individuals may wish to contact the University of Manitoba’s Office of Human Rights and Conflict Management. GCSS may refer individuals to this office if the offence is beyond the ability of GCSS to handle.

Potential consequences for violations include verbal warnings, issuing an apology to the affected party or parties, being banned permanently or temporarily from GCSS events, and being reported to the university.
